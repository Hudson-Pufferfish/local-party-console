# Party Games Guide - How to Play (1-8 Players)

No gaming experience is needed. Every game supports **1 to 8 people**. One person can play with AI-controlled players, called **bots**. In the lobby, bots can fill open places or be turned off. Some games need at least two total competitors, so a solo player must add at least one bot.

Your phone is the controller. Use the on-screen joystick by sliding your thumb in the direction you want to go. Tap a named button to use that action. A **cooldown** is the short wait before you can use an action again.

## Sumo Dash

**Players:** **1 to 8 people.** A solo player must add at least one bot because each match needs 2 to 8 total competitors. Bots can fill open places or be turned off.

### Goal

Stay on the floating ring and knock the other pucks off. Each round gives more points to players who stay on the ring longer. The match target is at least 3 points and grows with the number of competitors. In a two-competitor match, the first player to 3 points wins.

### Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Move joystick** | Steers your puck. The floor is slippery, so release the stick early when you want to stop. |
| **DASH** | Launches your puck in the direction it faces. It takes 1.5 seconds to recharge. |

### How a round works

- The match starts with a 30-second practice break. Later rounds have a 20-second break. You can move and dash, but nobody can fall out or score.
- After the `3, 2, 1` countdown, the round starts and the outer edge begins to close.
- Normal bumps push both pucks. A dash hit pushes much harder and briefly makes the other puck harder to steer. Two dashing pucks recoil from each other.
- The icy floor keeps pucks sliding. Near the edge, that extra movement can carry you out.
- The ring stays full size for 5 seconds. It then shrinks. If several players still remain, it pauses at a small size and then closes quickly until one player is left.
- If tied leaders reach the match target together, only those leaders play sudden death. The survivor gets the deciding point.

### Tips

- Aim a dash across the ring, not toward empty space outside it.
- Attack from the inside. A puck nearer the edge has less room to recover.
- Watch the dashed warning circle. It shows where the safe ring is moving.
- A dash is strongest as a planned hit. Do not spend it only to travel faster.

## Bomb Pass

**Players:** **1 to 8 people.** A solo player must add at least one bot because each match needs 2 to 8 total competitors. Bots can fill open places or be turned off.

### Goal

This is hot potato with a ticking bomb. Do not be holding the bomb when its fuse reaches zero. Survive longer than the other players to earn more points.

### Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Move joystick** | Steers your puck around the closed arena. |
| **DASH** | Gives a quick burst of speed for catching or escaping another player. It takes 1.5 seconds to recharge. |

### How a round works

- A random living player receives a bomb with a 15-second fuse.
- Touch another living puck to pass the bomb. The fuse does **not** reset.
- After a pass, the bomb cannot move again for 0.5 seconds. Use that short lockout to get away.
- The ticking, red warning, and phone vibration become more urgent with 5 seconds left and again with 2 seconds left.
- When the fuse ends, the holder is out. Any other puck within the 64-unit point-blank blast is also out.
- If at least two players remain, there is a 0.85-second breather and a fresh bomb appears. The round continues until one survivor remains.
- A blast that removes all final survivors gives no points. Those players replay a sudden-death round with a 7.5-second fuse.
- If tied match leaders reach the score target, only those contenders play until one takes the lead.

### Tips

- Do not chase in a straight line. Cut across the arena to meet the player you want to tag.
- When you receive the bomb, check the fuse before choosing a target.
- The holder moves slightly faster, but safe players can use the wall and their dash to escape.
- Move away immediately after a pass. The 0.5-second lockout prevents an instant pass back.

## Color Clash

**Players:** **1 to 8 people.** A solo player must add at least one bot because each match needs 2 to 8 total competitors. Bots can fill open places or be turned off.

### Goal

Roll over the floor to paint it in your color. You can paint blank tiles and paint over another player's tiles. The player with the most covered area when the 45-second round ends wins the round.

### Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Move joystick** | Steers your paint puck. Every tile under it changes to your color. |
| **BOOST** | Gives 3 seconds of higher speed and a much wider paint trail. The full cooldown is 8 seconds, so 5 seconds of recharge remain after the boost ends. |

### How a round works

- The square floor has 1,600 small paint tiles. Every active puck can overwrite any tile.
- A normal puck paints a narrow path. BOOST paints a wide splash and gives stronger shoves in collisions.
- Each scored round lasts 45 seconds. Nobody is knocked out.
- If first place is tied, the tied players enter a 30-second overtime. Other players stop moving, but their paint stays on the floor.
- A neutral golden tile appears during overtime. The first contender to touch it wins the round at once.
- If nobody reaches the golden tile, the contender with the most paint when overtime ends wins. The game has fixed tie rules if the totals still match.
- If tied leaders reach the match target, later rounds include only those leaders and award one deciding point.

### Tips

- Paint blank space early, then steal crowded enemy areas near the end.
- Save BOOST for a long open lane or a dense patch of enemy color.
- Turn during BOOST to make a wide arc instead of one straight stripe.
- In overtime, go to the golden tile. Protecting old paint is less important than the instant win.

## Last Light

**Players:** **1 to 8 people.** A solo player must add at least one bot because each match needs 2 to 8 total competitors. Bots can fill open places or be turned off.

### Goal

Last Light is a timing and nerve game. A lamp slowly dims and then blows out at a secret moment. Tap **LOCK IN** before the blackout. The latest valid tap scores the most points, but waiting too long scores nothing.

### Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **LOCK IN** | Records your choice for this lamp. You can tap only once. There is no movement control. |

### How a round works

- The scored blackout can happen from 3 to 12 seconds after the lamp starts. There is no visible timer, and the dimming does not reveal the exact moment.
- Tap before the lamp goes dark. A tap at or after the blackout is too late.
- Your phone shows `LOCKED` only after the host accepts the tap.
- Players who never locked in are out for that round and get zero points.
- Among players who locked in, the latest tap earns the most points. Earlier taps earn fewer points. Taps received on the exact same game step share the same rank.
- If nobody taps, the round is void and nobody scores.
- Tied leaders at the match target enter sudden death. Only those leaders play. A tie for the latest tap repeats sudden death without awarding a point.

### Tips

- An early tap is safe but usually scores less. A late tap can win the round or miss completely.
- Ignore small flickers. They create tension but do not reliably predict the blackout.
- Watch who has locked in, but remember that the screen hides their exact times until the reveal.
- Practice blackouts are shorter, from 1 to 3.5 seconds, and do not award match points.

## Citadel Defense

**Players:** **1 to 8 people.** You can play truly solo or add bots. With more people, bots can fill open places or be turned off. Everyone is on one team.

### Goal

Protect the central **Core** through 8 enemy waves. The whole squad wins by clearing wave 8. The whole squad loses if enemy attacks reduce Core health to zero.

### Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Move joystick** | Moves your defender and points it in a direction. |
| **Automatic fire** | Fires without a button at the threat closest to the Core inside the 120-degree cone in front of you. It deals 60 damage per second. An `AWAY` defender stays still but keeps firing in its last direction. |
| **A — LASER / RAIL** | Fires three piercing rays. Each deals 90 damage and 140 knockback. It breaks a Brute's front shield for 3 seconds. Cooldown: 2.5 seconds. |
| **B — DASH** | Moves at speed 680 for 0.16 seconds. Cooldown: 1.8 seconds. A body-check spins a shielded Brute around for 1 second so the squad can hit its back. |
| **X — WALL** | Places a wall with 180 health. It lasts 8 seconds, and each player can keep 2 walls active. Cooldown: 1.5 seconds. Walls stop enemies and block the boss laser. |
| **Y — REVIVE / BOMB** | Near a downed teammate, tap Y and stay within 64 pixels for 2.5 seconds to revive them with 60 health. Otherwise, inside the blue Core ring, tap Y to join the squad EMP charge. Revive always has priority. |

### Health, revive, and respawn

- Each player starts with 100 health.
- At zero health, a player is **downed** and can crawl slowly. In a normal squad, bleed-out lasts 15 seconds.
- If no teammate completes the revive, that player waits 8 seconds and then returns at the Core with 100 health.
- In solo or a very small weighted squad, bleed-out is 5 seconds and the respawn wait is 4 seconds.
- Each respawn costs 3% of the Core's maximum health. This cost is capped at 12% per wave and can never lower the Core below 1 health. Only an enemy can deliver the final damage.
- Between waves, there is a 15-second breather. The Core repairs 10% of its maximum health, all joined players return to 100 health, dead or downed players return at the Core, and LASER, DASH, and WALL cooldowns refresh.

### Squad EMP

- The EMP needs at least half of the living squad, including at least one human, to stand in the blue Core ring and tap Y.
- The group must charge it for 3 seconds. Leaving the ring, going down, or disconnecting removes that player from the count.
- The EMP can fire only once per wave.
- It deals 250 damage to drones and bosses. Bosses are also stunned for 1.5 seconds.
- It deals 125 damage to Brutes, breaks their shield, and stuns them for 4 seconds.
- The EMP cancels an active Dreadnought laser.

### Enemies

| Enemy | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Drone Swarm** | Light enemies that rush the Core. Fast Drones move even quicker. |
| **Shielded Brutes** | Heavy enemies with a front shield. Use LASER to break it or DASH to turn the Brute around. |
| **Drone Carriers** | Slow support enemies that release more drones. Remove them before the arena fills. |
| **Dreadnought** | The boss appears in wave 7. Wave 8 brings four bosses over time. Its red laser sweeps the arena, but a WALL stops the beam and protects players behind it. Front armor reduces normal damage. Below 33% health, each boss enters its fastest and most dangerous phase. An EMP cancels its laser. |

### Tips

- Face the danger you want automatic fire to hit. Movement and aim use the same direction.
- Put WALLS between the Core and a crowd, or between the squad and a sweeping red laser.
- Revive first when Y offers `REVIVE`. A living squad is more valuable than an early EMP.
- Save the once-per-wave EMP for a packed arena or an active boss laser.
- The Core cannot be destroyed by a respawn cost, but repeated respawns leave almost no room for the next enemy hit.
